Data Deletion

Last updated: November 2026

This page explains how to remove a connected platform's data from the D2C Analytics Dashboard ("the Dashboard"), operated by Probild.

Installed via the Shopify App Store? Uninstalling is enough

If you installed the Dashboard directly from the Shopify App Store, uninstalling the app from your Shopify Admin automatically deletes your account — your team member record, your store's connection, and every order record we synced from your store. This happens immediately via Shopify's own required data-protection webhook; you don't need to email us separately for this. Reinstalling afterward creates a fresh account.

Disconnecting a platform yourself

Any team member with access to a Client's settings can disconnect a connected platform (Shopify, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Shiprocket, or Delhivery) directly from the Dashboard, under Manage Clients → [Client] → Connected integrations → Disconnect. This immediately revokes the Dashboard's stored access token for that platform and stops any further syncing.

Requesting full data deletion (any other case)

For a Client set up outside the Shopify App Store path, or to request deletion of a single connected platform's data without removing the whole account, email admin@probild.in from an address associated with the Client account, including:

  1. The Client/store name as it appears in the Dashboard
  2. Which platform's data should be deleted (Shopify, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Shiprocket, or Delhivery)

We will confirm the request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

A deletion request removes the platform's stored access token and the order, campaign, or shipment records synced from that platform for the named Client. It does not affect data held directly by Shopify, Meta, Google, Shiprocket, or Delhivery themselves — those are separate systems the Client controls independently.
