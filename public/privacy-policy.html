Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the D2C Analytics Dashboard ("the Dashboard"), operated by Probild ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, and protects information when a business ("Client") connects its e-commerce and advertising accounts to the Dashboard.

The Dashboard is a business analytics tool. A Client's account is set up in one of two ways: (1) an existing Probild agency client, set up and managed by Probild's own team on the Client's behalf, or (2) a merchant who installs the Dashboard directly and self-serve from the Shopify App Store, which automatically creates the Client's account, connects their Shopify store, and signs them in — no manual setup by Probild required. Either way, the Dashboard itself is used by the Client's own authorized team members to view sales, advertising, and fulfilment performance in one place; it is a business tool, not a consumer-facing product, even though installation itself (for merchants using the Shopify App Store path) is self-serve and open to the public.

Information We Collect

When a Client connects a platform to the Dashboard, we access and store:

  • Shopify: order records (order date, amount, status, payment method, shipping city and state, product line items) and the customer name on each order. For Shopify App Store installs specifically, we also read the store's own name and contact email (a standard shop-level field, not a customer's) to create the Client's account.
  • Meta Ads and Google Ads: advertising account identifiers, campaign names and statuses, and aggregate performance metrics (spend, impressions, clicks, conversions). We do not access personal data about the people an ad was shown to.
  • Courier partners (Shiprocket, Delhivery): shipment and delivery status records tied to the orders above.
  • Account access tokens: the OAuth tokens each platform issues when a Client authorizes a connection, stored encrypted, used only to fetch the data described above on the Client's behalf.
  • Billing information: we do not ourselves store payment card numbers. Subscription billing is handled entirely by Razorpay or, for merchants who subscribe through the Shopify App Store, by Shopify's own Billing system — we store only the resulting subscription status and plan, not any card or bank details.

We only request read access to the data needed to power the Dashboard's analytics. We do not collect payment card numbers, passwords, or government ID information from any platform.

How We Use Information

  • To display sales, advertising, and fulfilment analytics to the Client and their authorized team members inside the Dashboard.
  • To keep that data current by periodically syncing with each connected platform.
  • To diagnose and fix problems with a Client's data or connections.

We do not sell this data, and we do not use it to serve ads or build profiles unrelated to the Dashboard's own analytics.

Data Sharing

We do not share Client data with third parties, except: the platforms the Client has themselves connected (to fetch the data back), the payment processors handling that Client's subscription (Razorpay, or Shopify's Billing system for App Store installs), and service providers who host our infrastructure — our application server, and Supabase, which hosts our database and handles account authentication — who only process data on our instructions.

Data Security

Account access tokens are encrypted at rest. Access to the Dashboard requires authentication, and a Client's data is only visible to that Client's own authorized team members and Probild's own team, based on each person's assigned access level.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain synced data for as long as a Client's account remains connected, so historical analytics stay available. A Client can disconnect any platform at any time from within the Dashboard, which stops further syncing.

Merchants who installed via the Shopify App Store: uninstalling the app from Shopify Admin automatically and promptly deletes that Client's account and all data we hold for it — no separate request needed. This happens through Shopify's own mandatory shop/redact webhook, which we implement to actually remove the data (the Client's account, team member records, and synced Shopify order data), not merely acknowledge the request.

For any other deletion request, or a Client set up outside the Shopify App Store path, see our Data Deletion instructions.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the Dashboard changes. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
